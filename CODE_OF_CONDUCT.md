# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Development Kit a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical and safety concerns.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing confidential information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption, deliberate disclosure of private information, and knowingly unsafe or malicious contributions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and interactions that violate this policy. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Development Kit project.

This code of conduct is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
